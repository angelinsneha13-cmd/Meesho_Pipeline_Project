## 2. Chart-Choice Justifications (No Images)

### Question 1: "Which month had the highest total revenue?"
- **Total Revenue Figures**: April = INR 419,417.43, May = INR 444,594.25, June = INR 398,055.24.
- **Recommended Chart Type**: Vertical Bar Chart (Discrete Temporal Comparison).
- **Justification**: This is a univariate discrete comparison across three distinct time periods. A vertical bar chart with the y-axis starting at zero allows readers to accurately evaluate the total revenue difference within 3 seconds without optical distortion. A line chart is avoided because three discrete monthly aggregates do not represent continuous trend tracking, and legends are omitted because there is only one data series.

### Question 2: "What percentage share does Ethnic Wear represent of April's total revenue?"
- **Revenue Figures**: INR 104,520.77 out of INR 419,417.43 (24.92%).
- **Recommended Chart Type**: 100% Stacked Bar or Donut Chart (Part-to-Whole Composition).
- **Justification**: This is a part-to-whole categorical composition metric. A 100% single stacked bar or clean donut chart highlights the proportion of total revenue generated by Ethnic Wear (24.92%) against all other categories combined (75.08%). The chart enforces a 0% baseline, eliminates 3D distortion, and uses direct slice labels instead of a detached legend to maximize immediate comprehension.

### Question 3: "How do the four regions compare on total revenue?"
- **Regional Figures**: North = INR 337,125.46, West = INR 333,106.33, South = INR 316,736.68, East = INR 275,098.45.
- **Recommended Chart Type**: Horizontal Bar Chart (Sorted Descending).
- **Justification**: This is a bivariate comparison between nominal categories (Regions) and an aggregated continuous measure (Revenue). A horizontal bar chart accommodates regional text labels naturally on the y-axis without angular tilt. Sorting bars descending from North down to East immediately guides the eye to regional rank within 2 seconds. The numerical axis starts strictly at zero, and legends are excluded since all bars share a single metric.
